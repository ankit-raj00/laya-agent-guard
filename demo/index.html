How it works

1 · One sheetThe question, the four labels and your text go into one input. Each label gets a [MASK] "answer box".
2 · ReadModernBERT-large reads everything bidirectionally; each box absorbs how well its label fits.
3 · CompareTwo extra Transformer layers let the boxes compete; one shared scorer gives each a number.
4 · CalibrateSoftmax with a fitted temperature turns scores into honest probabilities.

Fine-tuned on 78k examples from 13 public datasets plus synthetic indirect injections, on free Kaggle GPUs.
